## Safety weighting (this issue is labelled `safety`)

- Verification is really polled to confirmation, not assumed: `verifyOldestFirst` calls `editPlaylist`, reads the sort state back from that response, and only accepts it when `selectedOrder === 2`; otherwise it calls `browseWatchLater` and checks again, matching upstream's two-path check. Neither call is skipped and neither response is trusted blind.
- The scanner refuses positions when verification never lands: `scan` awaits `verifyOldestFirst` before touching `entries`, so an unverified sort throws before any page is fetched or any position is assigned. Test 1 asserts zero continuation calls in that case, which is the strongest form of "no positions returned".
- Missing data never matches: the scanner itself does not evaluate rules (that is `rules.js`, already reviewed under #8), so nothing here introduces a new missing-data path. `setVideoId` absence is the only field the scanner branches on (an entry with no `setVideoId` is silently skipped by `addEntries`, same drop-at-source behaviour `playlistParser.js` already established and tested at the parser level), and that check is unconditional, not a missing-data special case.

## Design note carried over from the plan

`extractSortState` is duplicated conceptually with the userscript's, but
implemented fresh here rather than imported, because the two live in
different files with different call shapes (`json` walk vs.
`innertube`-mediated response) and the scanner has no dependency on
`innertube.js` internals beyond its three public methods. This was a
deliberate decision recorded in the plan, not an oversight.
