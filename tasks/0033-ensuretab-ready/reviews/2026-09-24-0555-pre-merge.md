## Residual notes

- The browser spec's close-then-reconnect case needs an extra hop
  (`context.waitForEvent('page')` plus `newPage.goto(...)` from Node) that
  the rest of the spec does not, because `chrome.tabs.create`'s own first
  navigation is not observed by Playwright's `context.route` on this host
  and would otherwise hit the real network. Documented with a comment in
  the spec and in the progress log. Not a product bug, a test harness
  constraint specific to this sandbox's network path.
