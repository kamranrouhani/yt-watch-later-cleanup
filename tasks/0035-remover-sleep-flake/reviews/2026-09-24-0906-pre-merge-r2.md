## The rework in one paragraph

The single round 1 finding was that the timer-stub test pinned the pause
being scheduled but not being awaited. The rework keeps the approved
schedule assertion and adds the await pin: the stubbed timer sets a
`sleepResolved` flag inside its callback, and the fake innertube records,
synchronously at entry to its second `editPlaylist` call, whether the
flag was up. The green path is deterministic because `run()` resumes from
the sleep's resolve, which happens in the same timer callback that sets
the flag, so batch 2 cannot start before the flag is up. The unawaited
mutation is deterministic in the other direction: with `sleep(pauseMs)`
unawaited, batch 2 starts in the same microtask turn as batch 1, before
any timer callback can run, so the flag is down and the assertion fails.
No wall-clock reads anywhere in the test.
